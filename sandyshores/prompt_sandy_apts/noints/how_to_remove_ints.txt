If you do not want to have insides of the apartments you can use "non interior version"

Follow these steps:

1. Copy files from this current folder
2. Paste them into "prompt_sandy_apts\stream\replace_if_no_ints"
3. Go into the "config.lua" and change "Config.ProximityEnabled = true" to be "false".
4. That's it.

If you also want to use shells, the names of the models to spawn are:

1. prompt_sandy_apts_shell_1bd
2. prompt_sandy_apts_shell_2bd
